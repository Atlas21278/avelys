# Surface brief — Accueil public (FR `/`, EN `/en`)

Scope: public home and the shared design system it establishes (VTC-012 tokens and components, VTC-014 page). Mode: **Persuade**.
Audience and job: an international tourist, on a phone, planning a Paris arrival; must understand in seconds that two named founders will drive them at a fixed price known before booking, in an electric car, and start a quote.
Proof available: none yet (no photos, no reviews). Image slots stay labelled provisional. No invented claims.
Unresolved: prices (DEC-03), contact channel (DEC-20), legal mentions (DEC-08), logo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: every trip is shown as a travel-guide itinerary — numbered stops, distance, duration, fixed price — read at a glance. Refuses the category default: black sedan at night under a gold serif.

OWN-WORLD: anthracite cloth binding (#1F2124) as the only dark field, ivory matte pages (#F4EFE6), graphite annotation grey, a champagne ribbon bookmark (#B8996A) that marks the current place and nothing else. Bodoni Moda for place names and titles, Schibsted Grotesk for reference text and lining figures (its tabular comma is too wide). Square printed corners, 1px rules, route lines solid when chosen and dotted when merely possible; the price owns a fixed, drawn slot even before it exists.

STORY: the visitor sees their own route already laid out like a guide page, learns who drives and that the price is fixed, then asks for the price.

FIRST VIEWPORT: on phone, the promise set in large Bodoni over two lines, then the itinerary card (départ → destination as a numbered route line, date, passengers) with the empty price slot and the ink "Voir le prix" button inside the thumb zone. On desktop the promise sits in the left column, the itinerary card as a guide page on the right, the ribbon bookmark hanging from its top edge. Signature interaction: when a destination is chosen, the dotted route line draws itself solid, once.

FORM: Le Guide de voyage (Guides Bleus / guide vert itineraries), position 6 of the ordered list, seed key 641ab34b.

Raised: fixed price slot drawn while empty (from seven-segment display); committed choice solid, alternatives dotted (from sewing-pattern envelope); one dominant word per section as its door (from street typography).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
